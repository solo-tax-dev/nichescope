Etsy Message Templates for Customers: A Practical Guide

Good customer communication on Etsy does not need to be flowery or time-consuming. A short, clear message sent at the right moment builds trust, reduces support requests, and can quietly improve your review rate. This guide covers the messages worth sending, what to put in them, and some ready-to-adapt templates you can copy today.

Why Bother With Etsy Message Templates?

Typing the same reply from scratch every day is a drain. Templates let you respond consistently without sounding like a robot. They also reduce the chance of forgetting something important — a shipping timeframe, a care instruction, or a simple thank you that makes a buyer feel looked after.

There is a second, less obvious reason. Buyers who feel informed are less likely to open a case or leave a frustrated review. A proactive message costs you thirty seconds to set up and can save a much longer conversation later.

The Four Messages Worth Automating

1. Order Confirmation

Etsy sends its own automated receipt, but a personal note from your shop adds something Etsy's receipt cannot: your voice. Keep this short. The buyer has just handed over money; reassure them it was a good decision.

Template:

Hi [Name], thank you so much for your order. I have got it and will begin [making / picking / preparing] it straight away. Expected dispatch is [timeframe]. If you have any questions before then, just message me here. [Your name]

2. Dispatch Notification

Etsy marks an order as dispatched when you add tracking, but a personal message on top of that removes ambiguity. It also sets expectations about delivery windows, which is where a lot of buyer anxiety lives.

Template:

Hi [Name], your order is on its way. I posted it [today / this morning] via [Royal Mail / USPS / courier name]. You should receive it within [X working days], though around busy periods it can occasionally take a little longer. Your tracking number is [number] if you would like to follow it. Let me know if anything seems wrong when it arrives. [Your name]

3. Post-Delivery Follow-Up

This one is optional but underused. A short message a few days after the estimated delivery — checking everything arrived safely — shows care without being pushy. Do not ask for a review directly; Etsy discourages any pressure around reviews. Just ask if everything is okay.

Template:

Hi [Name], I hope your order arrived safely and that you are happy with it. If anything is not quite right, please message me and I will sort it out. I hope you enjoy it. [Your name]

4. Custom Order Acknowledgement

Custom orders carry more risk for both sides. A clear message confirming exactly what has been agreed prevents expensive misunderstandings. Spell out every detail: colour, size, wording, personalisation, delivery date.

Template:

Hi [Name], thank you for your custom order request. Just to confirm the details before I start: [list every specification here]. If everything looks correct, please confirm and I will get started. Expected completion and dispatch is [date]. Let me know if you would like to change anything at this stage. [Your name]

Handling Common Incoming Messages

Beyond outgoing templates, it is worth having a short library of replies to frequent questions. These do not need to be long — just accurate and friendly.

Where Is My Order?

Template:

Hi [Name], thanks for getting in touch. Your order was dispatched on [date] via [service]. Tracking shows [status] — the link is [tracking URL]. If it has not arrived by [date], please message me again and I will investigate with the carrier. Sorry for the wait. [Your name]

Can I Change My Order?

Template:

Hi [Name], thanks for letting me know. If your order has not gone into production yet, I should be able to change [detail]. I will check and come back to you within [timeframe]. If production has already started, changes may not be possible, but I will do my best. [Your name]

Something Arrived Damaged

Template:

Hi [Name], I am really sorry to hear that. Could you send me a photo of the damage so I can see what happened? Once I have that I will arrange a replacement [or refund] for you as quickly as possible. Thank you for letting me know. [Your name]

How to Set Up Saved Replies in Etsy

Etsy has a built-in saved replies feature inside your Messages section. To set one up, go to your Etsy shop manager, click Messages, open a conversation, and look for the saved reply icon in the message box. You can store multiple templates there and insert them with a click. You can still edit before sending, so personalise anything in square brackets before you hit send.

For your automated order confirmation, go to Shop Manager, then Settings, then Info and Appearance. There is a field called Message to Buyers that sends automatically with every order receipt. Keep this brief — it is not a substitute for a personal message, but it is a good place to put your dispatch timeframe and a note that buyers can message you with any questions.

Tone: What Works and What Sounds Off

Plain, warm, and specific beats enthusiastic and vague every time. Phrases like 'I am so excited to create your magical piece!' can read as insincere if the buyer is a repeat customer ordering something practical. Stick to what you actually mean.

  • Use the buyer's first name — Etsy shows it on every order.
  • Mention the specific item they bought, not just 'your order'.
  • Give a concrete timeframe rather than 'soon'.
  • One warm sign-off is enough — avoid multiple exclamation marks.

If English is not your first language, simpler sentences are better. Buyers appreciate clarity more than elaborate phrasing.

What Messaging Has to Do With Your Shop Performance

Etsy's Star Seller badge requires a message response rate of 95% or above within 24 hours. That applies to the first message in a new conversation. Saved reply templates make hitting that target much easier — you can send a brief holding reply within minutes and follow up with full detail later.

Beyond the badge, fast and clear responses influence reviews, repeat purchases, and word of mouth. They also reduce cases opened through Etsy — and fewer cases means a cleaner shop record.

A Few Things to Avoid

  • Do not ask for a review in your message template. Etsy prohibits asking buyers to leave only positive reviews, and even a neutral ask can feel pressured. Your post-delivery follow-up asking if everything arrived safely will often prompt a review naturally.
  • Do not send too many messages. An order confirmation and a dispatch notification are standard. More than that starts to feel like spam.
  • Do not copy-paste without checking. Leaving [Name] unsub stituted or sending the wrong tracking number damages trust more than no message at all.
  • Do not use templates as a substitute for actually reading the buyer's message. If someone has asked a specific question, answer it specifically.

Quick Template Summary

  • Order confirmation — reassure, give expected dispatch timeframe
  • Dispatch notification — confirm postal method, give tracking, set delivery expectation
  • Post-delivery follow-up — check everything arrived, invite contact if not
  • Custom order acknowledgement — confirm every specification before starting
  • Where is my order — give tracking status, set a date to follow up if needed
  • Change request — acknowledge, check status, give honest answer
  • Damaged item — apologise, ask for photo, commit to resolution

If you use these as a starting point and adjust the language to match how you actually speak, they will feel natural rather than automated — which is exactly the effect you want.

FAQ: Etsy Message Templates for Customers

Does Etsy have built-in message templates?

Yes. Etsy has a saved replies feature inside the Messages section of your shop manager. You can save multiple templates and insert them into any conversation with a single click, then edit before sending. There is also a separate Message to Buyers field in your shop settings that sends automatically with every order confirmation.

Can I ask customers to leave a review in my message?

Etsy's policies prohibit asking buyers to leave only positive reviews, and strongly discourage any messaging that pressures buyers around reviews. A post-delivery message checking that everything arrived safely will often result in a review naturally, without you having to ask directly.

How do message templates help with Star Seller status?

Star Seller requires a 95% or above first-response rate within 24 hours. Having a saved reply ready means you can send a short acknowledgement within minutes of receiving a message, even if your full answer takes longer to compose. That quick first reply is what Etsy measures for the badge.

How personal should an Etsy message template be?

Templates work best as a starting structure, not a word-for-word script you send unchanged. At minimum, use the buyer's first name and mention the specific item they ordered. For custom work, include the exact specifications you have agreed. A template that sounds specific to the buyer builds more trust than one that is obviously copied from a standard form.
