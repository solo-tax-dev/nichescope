How Much Does EverBee Cost? Pricing, Plans and What You Get

If you have been looking at Etsy research tools, EverBee has probably come up. Before you commit to a subscription, it is worth understanding exactly what you are paying for, what the free tier actually lets you do, and whether the paid plans deliver enough value to justify the cost. This article walks through all of that in plain language.

EverBee Pricing: The Plans at a Glance

EverBee offers a free plan and two paid tiers. Pricing can change, so always check the EverBee website for the current figures, but the general structure has been:

  • Free plan — limited searches per month, access to the core Chrome extension features, estimated product data
  • Hobby plan — a monthly or annual subscription that unlocks more searches and some additional features
  • Pro plan — the highest tier, aimed at sellers who want shop analysis, keyword tools and deeper reporting

Paid plans are typically billed monthly or at a discount if you pay annually. The exact price points shift from time to time, so it is worth checking directly rather than trusting a number quoted in a third-party article written months ago.

What Does the Free Plan Actually Give You?

The EverBee free plan lets you install the Chrome extension and run a limited number of product searches each month. Once you hit the cap, you are prompted to upgrade. For someone who is just dipping a toe in, the free plan is enough to get a feel for the interface.

The core thing EverBee shows you — on any plan — is estimated sales volume and estimated revenue for Etsy listings. These figures are EverBee's own calculations, not numbers published by Etsy. Etsy does not share sales data publicly, so any tool that shows you a sales count or a revenue figure is working from an estimate, not a direct feed. That is worth keeping in mind when you are deciding how much weight to give the numbers.

What Do the Paid Plans Add?

Moving up to a paid plan generally unlocks:

  • More product searches per month (or unlimited, depending on the tier)
  • Shop-level analysis — estimated revenue and sales across a whole Etsy shop
  • Keyword research features
  • Tag and listing optimisation suggestions
  • Access to trend data and category breakdowns

The Hobby plan sits in the middle — more than free, but without some of the deeper shop analytics that come with Pro. For a seller who wants to check competition on a regular basis, Hobby is the plan most commonly considered. Pro is aimed at more active sellers or researchers who want to analyse competitors at the shop level regularly.

Is EverBee Worth the Monthly Cost?

That depends almost entirely on how much you trust the estimated figures it is built on.

EverBee calculates sales and revenue by working backwards from review counts and other visible signals. The methodology is not published in full, and Etsy does not verify or endorse the numbers. Some sellers find the estimates directionally useful — good enough to spot whether a niche is busy or quiet. Others have found the figures misleading enough that they made product or pricing decisions they later regretted.

If you are paying a monthly fee for data, it is reasonable to ask: how accurate is this, really? The honest answer is that nobody outside of Etsy knows for certain, because the underlying sales data is private.

What Is Included in the Annual Discount?

Like most SaaS tools, EverBee offers a meaningful discount if you pay for a full year upfront rather than month by month. The saving is usually somewhere in the range of 30 to 40 percent compared to paying monthly, though again the exact figures can change. If you have already tested the tool and are confident it fits your workflow, the annual plan reduces the overall cost. If you are still evaluating, paying monthly first gives you a lower-risk exit.

How EverBee Compares to Other Etsy Research Tools on Price

EverBee sits in a similar bracket to eRank and Alura when it comes to pricing structure — all three offer a free tier with limits, and paid plans in roughly the same monthly range. The differences come down to what each tool emphasises: eRank leans heavily into keyword and SEO data, Alura covers listing optimisation and email tools, and EverBee is most associated with its estimated sales and revenue figures.

All of these tools show estimated data for sales-related metrics because, again, that data is not available from Etsy. That is not a criticism of any specific product — it is just the reality of what is publicly accessible.

Before You Pay for EverBee: A Practical Checklist

  • Have you used the free plan long enough to test accuracy? Run a few searches on products you already know sell well and see whether the estimates feel plausible.
  • Do you need shop-level analysis? If yes, that pushes you toward the paid tier. If you only need product-level research, the free plan or a free alternative may cover you.
  • How often will you actually use it? A monthly subscription only makes sense if you are doing research regularly. Occasional sellers often find the free tier is enough.
  • Are you comfortable with estimated data? If you want to make decisions based on figures you can verify, look at what is genuinely visible on the page rather than relying solely on calculated estimates.

The Bottom Line on EverBee Costs

EverBee is not expensive by SaaS standards, but it is not free either once you need more than the basic monthly search limit. The free plan is genuinely useful for getting started, and the paid plans add meaningful features if you use them consistently. The question of whether it is worth it comes down to how much you trust estimated sales data and how central that data is to your research process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EverBee completely free?

EverBee has a free plan that lets you run a limited number of searches each month. Once you hit that limit you will need to upgrade to a paid plan to continue. The free tier is enough to evaluate the tool but is not designed for heavy regular use.

How accurate are EverBee's sales estimates?

EverBee calculates estimated sales from signals like review counts rather than from data Etsy shares directly, because Etsy does not publish sales figures publicly. The estimates can be useful as a rough directional guide but should not be treated as precise figures. No third-party tool has access to verified sales data from Etsy.

Can I do Etsy competition research without paying for a tool?

Yes. A lot of useful competitive information is already visible on Etsy search pages — prices, review counts, how many listings appear, and how many are ads. Free tools that surface this on-page data can give you a solid foundation for niche research without any subscription cost.

What is the difference between EverBee Hobby and Pro?

The Hobby plan unlocks more monthly searches than the free tier and covers most core product research features. The Pro plan adds higher usage limits, deeper shop-level analysis and more advanced keyword and reporting tools. If you only need to check product-level competition occasionally, Hobby is usually sufficient. Pro is aimed at sellers doing frequent, in-depth competitor research.
