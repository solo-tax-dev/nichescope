How Much Is Etsy Plus — and Is It Worth It?

Etsy Plus costs $10 USD per month (billed monthly, no annual option). That converts to roughly £8–£9 for UK sellers, though Etsy bills in USD regardless of where you are, so the exact sterling amount shifts with the exchange rate.

That is the honest, direct answer. The rest of this article covers what you actually get for that money, what you do not get, and how to decide whether it makes sense for your shop.

What Etsy Plus Includes

Etsy Plus bundles a handful of features that do not exist on the free plan. Here is what is currently included:

  • 15 listing credits per month — each credit covers one listing renewal (normally $0.20 each, so roughly $3 of value).
  • $5 USD in Etsy Ads credit per month — applied automatically to your ads balance.
  • Restock requests — lets shoppers sign up to be notified when a sold-out item comes back.
  • Advanced shop customisation — extra banner options and the ability to feature listings in a more prominent layout.
  • Discounted custom web address — access to a custom domain through Hover at a reduced first-year price.
  • Discounted custom packaging and merchandise — partner deals for boxes, labels and similar items.

That is the full list. Nothing hidden, nothing extra.

What Etsy Plus Does Not Include

This is where many sellers feel let down, so it is worth being explicit.

Etsy Plus does not give you better search ranking. Your listings do not get priority placement because you pay for Plus. Etsy has confirmed this repeatedly — organic search position is determined by the same algorithm for all sellers.

It does not give you access to additional analytics beyond what is already in Etsy's free seller dashboard. You get the same traffic data, the same conversion figures, the same search term report.

It does not remove Etsy transaction fees, listing fees beyond the included credits, or payment processing fees. Those costs continue exactly as before.

And it does not unlock any research or competitive intelligence features. If you want to understand how your niche is priced or how many competitors you are up against, Etsy Plus does nothing to help with that.

Is Etsy Plus Worth the Money?

The honest way to think about this is to add up the concrete credits and compare them to $10.

You get $3 in listing credits and $5 in ads credit — that is $8 of direct monetary value each month, assuming you actually use both. If you run ads and regularly renew listings, you recover most of the fee. If you do not run ads (many sellers prefer not to), the ads credit evaporates unused, and the real monthly value drops to just the listing credits.

The restock request feature has genuine value if you sell physical items that regularly sell out — it keeps interested buyers in the loop without you doing anything manually. For digital sellers or shops with consistent stock, it is largely irrelevant.

The custom domain discount sounds appealing but is a one-time first-year saving on a product many sellers never need. The banner and customisation upgrades are minor visual changes that most shoppers will not notice.

So the realistic calculation looks something like this:

  • If you spend at least $5 a month on Etsy Ads and regularly renew listings: Etsy Plus likely pays for itself.
  • If you do not run ads: you are paying $10 to get $3 back in listing credits plus some light shop customisation.
  • If you are just starting out and validating a product idea: the subscription fee is almost certainly not the right place to spend money yet.

How Etsy Plus Compares to the Free Plan

For most new sellers, the free Etsy seller account is entirely adequate. You can list products, run a shop, access your stats, and sell internationally without paying anything beyond the standard per-transaction fees.

The free plan does not restrict how many listings you can have, how many sales you can make, or which categories you can sell in. There is no artificial ceiling that Etsy Plus unlocks.

Established sellers with higher listing volumes and active ad campaigns are the clearest candidates for Plus. For everyone else, it is optional.

What Actually Moves the Needle for Etsy Sellers

Paying $10 a month for Etsy Plus will not fix a listing that is poorly optimised, priced wrong for the market, or competing in a space that is already saturated. Those are the real levers.

Before worrying about subscriptions, it is worth understanding:

  • What prices competitors are charging — not estimated revenue, but the actual listed prices you can see on the page.
  • How many reviews competing listings have — which tells you roughly how established the competition is.
  • How much of the search results page is occupied by paid ads — a high ad density suggests sellers are competing hard for that keyword, which affects how much organic visibility is realistically available.

Frequently Asked Questions About Etsy Plus

Can I cancel Etsy Plus at any time?

Yes. Etsy Plus is a monthly subscription with no lock-in. You can cancel before your next billing date and you will not be charged again. Any unused credits from the current billing cycle are not refunded, but you keep access to Plus features until the end of the period you have paid for.

Does Etsy Plus help with SEO or search ranking?

No. Etsy has been clear that paying for Plus does not affect where your listings appear in search results. Ranking is determined by relevance, listing quality score, review history, and other factors that apply equally to free and Plus accounts.

Is there a free trial for Etsy Plus?

Etsy has offered free trials in the past, but this changes periodically. Check your shop manager directly — if a trial is available to your account, it will be shown there. There is no guaranteed ongoing free trial as of the time of writing.

What is a better use of $10 a month for a new Etsy seller?

For most new sellers, the priority is understanding whether your product and pricing make sense before spending money on anything. Research your niche — look at real prices, review counts, and competition levels on actual search results pages — before committing to subscriptions. Once you are selling consistently and running ads, Etsy Plus becomes more worth evaluating. Until then, the free seller account and free research tools are enough to get started.

The Short Answer

Etsy Plus costs $10 a month. It gives you $8 in direct credits (listing renewals and ads) if you use both, plus restock notifications and minor shop customisation. It does not improve your search ranking, give you extra analytics, or reduce your transaction fees.

If you run ads and have a steady shop, it roughly pays for itself. If you are starting out or not running ads, the free Etsy plan is almost certainly enough — and spending time on research and pricing strategy will do more for your shop than any subscription.
