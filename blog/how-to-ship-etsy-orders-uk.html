How to Ship Etsy Orders UK: A Plain Practical Guide

Shipping is one of those parts of running an Etsy shop that sounds straightforward until you actually have to do it. Which courier? How much to charge? What goes on the customs form? This guide covers the practical side of shipping Etsy orders from the UK, without the waffle.

Understanding Your Shipping Options in the UK

UK sellers have several realistic options for posting Etsy orders. The main ones are Royal Mail, Evri (formerly Hermes), DPD, and Parcelforce. Each has its place depending on what you are sending and where it is going.

Royal Mail

Royal Mail is the default choice for most small Etsy sellers, and for good reason. It is widely trusted by buyers, has the most granular size-and-weight pricing of any UK carrier, and handles everything from a small envelope to a large parcel. For lightweight items — jewellery, prints, small gifts — Royal Mail Large Letter or Small Parcel rates are very competitive. Click and Drop is their free platform for buying postage in bulk and printing labels at home, which saves time and usually gives a small discount over counter prices.

Evri

Evri works best for heavier or bulkier items where Royal Mail pricing starts climbing. Their rates for parcels over 1 kg are often noticeably cheaper. The trade-off is that buyer perception of Evri is more mixed, so it is worth considering what you are selling and who your customers are likely to be. For low-value bulky goods it can make commercial sense.

DPD and Parcelforce

These are worth considering once your order volume grows and you can negotiate account rates, or when you need tracked next-day delivery for higher-value items. They are less relevant for sellers just starting out.

Setting Up Shipping Profiles on Etsy

Etsy lets you create shipping profiles so you do not have to re-enter postage details for every listing. If you sell multiple items with similar sizes and weights, setting up two or three profiles (say, one for small letters, one for large letters, one for small parcels) saves a significant amount of time.

Within each profile you set a price for domestic UK delivery and can add international zones separately. You can also offer free shipping, where Etsy builds the postage cost into the item price for display purposes — this can lift search visibility slightly, though it is not a guarantee of better ranking.

How Much to Charge for Shipping

There are broadly two approaches: charge actual cost, or offer free shipping by folding cost into the item price.

Charging actual cost is transparent and avoids you eating into margin, but buyers sometimes abandon carts when they see postage added at checkout. Free shipping removes that friction but requires you to price the item correctly to begin with. Neither is universally better — it depends on your margins, your category, and what your competition is doing.

To see what competitors are actually charging, search your product term on Etsy and look at listed prices and whether shipping is shown separately. A common mistake is estimating postage rather than measuring it. Royal Mail pricing is based on both weight and dimensions — a lightweight but bulky item might cost more than you expect because it exceeds a size tier. Weigh and measure your packaged items before setting prices.

Packaging Considerations

Good packaging does two things: it protects the item in transit, and it contributes to the unboxing experience your buyer has. Both matter on Etsy.

For flat items like art prints, use rigid board-backed envelopes or sandwich the print between two pieces of stiff card inside an envelope. For fragile items, wrap in bubble wrap and use a box with enough void fill to stop movement. Jewellery typically ships well in a small padded envelope or a gift box inside a padded bag.

Weight adds up fast. Heavy packaging eats your margin if you are paying by weight, so look for packaging that is protective but not unnecessarily dense. Tissue paper, for example, adds a premium feel with almost no weight penalty.

Tracked vs Untracked Postage

Tracking is not compulsory for Etsy sales, but it protects you in disputes. If a buyer opens a case saying their order never arrived, having tracking evidence is often the difference between a resolved case and a refund you have to absorb.

For low-value items the cost of tracked postage may not be worth it — Royal Mail Tracked 48 costs meaningfully more than standard Second Class. Many sellers use untracked for orders under a certain value and switch to tracked above it. That threshold is a judgement call based on your margins.

For Star Seller status, Etsy measures your dispatch rate (whether you mark items as shipped on time), not necessarily whether you have a tracking number. Marking an order dispatched in Etsy when you post it — even without a tracking number — counts toward that metric.

Shipping Internationally from the UK

Selling to buyers outside the UK involves a few extra steps.

Customs forms

Any parcel leaving the UK needs a customs declaration. If you use Click and Drop with Royal Mail, the customs information is generated as part of the label process. You will need to provide a description of the contents, a value, and a commodity (HS tariff) code. Keep descriptions accurate — writing vague things like 'gift' can cause delays for the buyer.

VAT and import duties

For sales to the EU, Etsy collects and remits VAT on orders under the relevant threshold through their Import One Stop Shop registration. This means for lower-value orders you do not need to register for EU VAT yourself. For higher-value orders or sales to countries outside Etsy's VAT collection coverage, the buyer may face import charges on delivery. It is good practice to mention this in your shop policies so buyers are not surprised.

US sales

Etsy similarly handles sales tax collection for US buyers in most states, so again you do not need to worry about registering. Your responsibility is accurate customs documentation.

Handling Returns and Damaged Items

Set your return policy clearly in your shop policies before you start selling. Etsy gives you template options (no returns, 30-day returns, etc.) but you can customise the detail. Being clear upfront reduces disputes later.

If an item arrives damaged, Royal Mail has a compensation process for items sent via their tracked or special delivery services. Keep proof of postage and photographs of your packaging before dispatch if you regularly send fragile items — these help any claims.

FAQ: Shipping Etsy Orders in the UK

Do I need to use Etsy's postage labels or can I arrange my own?

You can arrange postage entirely independently of Etsy — buy stamps at the post office, use Click and Drop, or any courier you choose. Etsy does offer postage label purchase through their platform in some regions, but UK sellers typically find it more flexible to use Royal Mail Click and Drop or a courier dashboard directly. Whichever method you use, mark the order as dispatched in Etsy so the buyer is notified and your dispatch metrics are recorded correctly.

What size counts as a large letter with Royal Mail?

Royal Mail's large letter format has a maximum size of 353 x 250 x 25mm and a maximum weight of 750g. It is significantly cheaper than the small parcel rate, so it is worth engineering your packaging to fit within these dimensions if possible. Even a millimetre over the thickness limit will move the item into parcel pricing at the counter.

Can I offer free shipping on Etsy UK and still make a profit?

Yes, but you need to build the postage cost into your item price accurately. The risk is under-estimating postage, especially for heavier items or international orders. Work out your all-in cost (item cost plus actual postage plus Etsy fees) and price from there. Free shipping can reduce checkout abandonment, but it only works if your prices are set correctly from the start.

Do I need to fill in a customs form for orders to the Republic of Ireland?

Yes. Since Brexit, the Republic of Ireland is treated as an international destination for customs purposes. Parcels sent from Great Britain to Ireland need a customs declaration, just like any other EU country. Northern Ireland has different rules because of the Windsor Framework, so if you are based in Northern Ireland the position differs — check the Royal Mail guidance for your specific situation.
