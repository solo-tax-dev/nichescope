How these fees are calculated

Etsy charges a fixed $0.20 listing fee per item, whether or not it sells. On top of that, a 6.5% transaction fee applies to the full order — item price plus shipping and any gift wrap — regardless of where you're based.

Payment processing is charged separately and varies by your payment account's country:

CountryProcessing fee
United States3% + $0.25 per order
United Kingdom4% + £0.20 per order
Eurozone4% + €0.30 per order

If a sale is attributed to an Offsite Ad (Etsy advertising your listing on Google, Facebook, Instagram or Pinterest), it replaces the transaction fee with a flat 15% charge — dropping to 12% once your shop has made $10,000 or more in the trailing 12 months, at which point enrollment also becomes mandatory rather than optional.

This tool applies Etsy's own published fee policy to the numbers you enter. It isn't affiliated with Etsy, and Etsy can change rates at any time — always confirm against your own Shop Manager for exact figures.
