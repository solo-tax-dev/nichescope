Nichescope

Tracking product prices,
so the right moment isn't missed.

A personal tool for monitoring product prices across UK retailers, built to spot when something is genuinely worth buying rather than trusting a sale banner at face value.

What it does

Price history, not price hype

Nichescope watches product listings across a set of UK retailers and keeps a running record of what things actually cost over time. Retail prices move constantly — a "sale" price is sometimes just the normal price with a red sticker on it. This tool exists to tell the difference.

It pulls in retailer product feeds, tracks price movements per item, and flags when a current price sits meaningfully below its recent trend.

How it's built

Small, personal, and quiet

Feeds Reads official retailer product data — no scraping of retailer websites directly.
Local Runs on a personal schedule, not a public-facing storefront or search engine.
Alerts Sends a private notification when a tracked item's price drops notably.
Solo Built and used by one person, not a team or a public product.

In short

A price tracker, honestly described

  • Not a public comparison shopping site — no visitor-facing search or listings.
  • Uses retailer-provided product data feeds, accessed through standard affiliate/partner channels.
  • Built for personal price research: knowing when a price is actually good.
